End-of-Turn Detection — Solution Summary

Result (honest, grouped out-of-fold): mean response delay at ≤5% false-cutoff rate improves from 1600 ms → 1255 ms (English) and 850 ms → 840 ms (Hindi) vs the silence-only baseline, with ranking quality AUC 0.5 → 0.66 (en) / 0.70 (hi) (best single-model AUCs during development: 0.70/0.74). The shipped predictions_*.csv score 264/375 ms in-sample because the final model is trained on all provided turns — the OOF numbers above are the claim, not the memorized ones.

1 · Why the status quo fails

A silence-only endpointer must outwait the longest hesitations. English hold pauses reach 3.0 s (median 0.4 s), so the timer lands at 1600 ms. Hindi holds are shorter (p90 = 0.63 s), which is why the naive baseline already sits at 850 ms — and why beating it there is much harder.

2 · What the model does

For every pause it scores p_eot from audio strictly before pause_start:

blockcontent
anchored prosody (30)final pitch level/slope in semitones re speaker median, energy decay into the pause, final-voiced-run lengthening, tail voicing fraction, centroid trail-off, filler-flatness flag — all anchored at the true speech offset, not at the (lagging) pause_start annotation
log-mel tail (240)40-band log-mel over the last 1 s of actual speech, causal cepstral-mean-normalized, mean/std/delta pooled
turn contextpause index, time elapsed, previous-pause durations (past information), plus within-turn deltas: is this tail lower/quieter/longer than every earlier tail of the same speaker?
classifierprobability-averaged ensemble: logistic regression + 2 gradient boosters + small MLP, trained on English+Hindi jointly (per-language models lose — 248 samples each); holds <0.4 s are dropped from training because the metric cannot punish firing on them

Listening→model loop: the thud finding became a shipped rule: if the prefix contains a single <0.6s burst with <10% of its energy above 1 kHz (a thud rings periodically — it fools a pitch tracker — but has no high-frequency speech content), p_eot is capped at 0.02. It removes 7 English pre-speech false-fire holds (1294→1255 ms) at the cost of two very quiet one-word turn-ends (Hindi 830→840 ms) — for a live agent that trade is a slower response on rare turns, never an interruption before the user has spoken a word.

7 · Causality

Every feature reads audio[0 : pause_start] only (eot_features.py, mel_features.py). Per-speaker normalizers (median pitch, energy floor) come from that same prefix. pause_end of the current pause is never touched; previous pauses' boundaries are past events a live agent has already observed. Hold durations are used only as training-time sample filters (label-side information), never as inference features.

8 · Human vs coding agent

Error analysis by ear — the highest-leverage work in this project. Two listening rounds over 14 flagged worst-error clips produced diagnoses the statistics had misread: (1) en__091's 3s "hold" is a thud + silence before the speaker's first word — this finding became the shipped pre-speech spectral guard (−39 ms English) after the agent's pitch tracker had been fooled by the thud's periodic ring; (2) en__064 is a second pre-speech case that slips past the guard (two noise bursts), defining guard v2; (3) hi__036's speaker is shouting in anger — high-arousal prosody explains 3 of the 8 Hindi blocking holds and exposed a flaw in median-based per-turn normalization; (4) en__030 reads a phone number in digit groups, confirming the list-rhythm failure mode; (5) en__080/en__007 pause after "it is…"/"and…" — establishing the lexical boundary of acoustic-only methods. Also: direction and judgment calls throughout — the metric-first development strategy, the decision to train jointly on both languages, the freeze when pYIN proved unshippable, commissioning the literature-driven second round (VAP-style self-supervision), and the deadline ship/no-ship call documented in RUNLOG rows 13–19. Implementation and iteration mechanics under the human's direction: feature extractors, the pause_start-lag instrumentation, mel pipeline, aux models, metric-aware training, ensembling, scoring harness, plots, and this report.

10 · Second research round (documented, not shipped)

After submission-quality was locked, a literature-driven round (Voice Activity Projection, Ekstedt & Skantze 2022) added: a self-supervised future-VAD model ("does speech resume within H ms?") trained fold-safely on 142k unlabeled frames of the provided audio; a temporal sliced-mel variant at speech offsets; yin-quality pitch on anchored tails (strongest single Hindi features, slope AUC 0.676); and within-turn stage-2 recalibration. Best honest config — one GBM over all blocks + recalibration — reaches 1159 ms English OOF (Hindi stays ~850: its ~8 blocking holds are cue-less to human listeners too — lexical completion, unreachable without ASR). Not integrated into predict.py under deadline (requires shipping aux models + a 0.3 s/pause tracker); it is the validated first step of the roadmap. Full details in RUNLOG.md rows 13–19.
